# H1 RSI Extreme Oversold Context for V4.2 (1 June 2026)

## The Event
On 1 June 2026, GOLD reached $4,494.78 on H1 with **RSI(14) = 15.3** — extreme oversold territory.

## What RSI < 20 means for V4.2

V4.2 is a trend-following scalper with **no RSI filter** — it will continue to generate SELL signals on M1 EMA20 breakouts as long as the H1 trend is DOWN.

But RSI < 20 on H1 is rare and has implications:

1. **Probability of snap-back rally increases** — RSI below 20 historically predicts mean reversion within 3-5 candles
2. **Volume thins out near support** — fewer breakouts, more fakeouts
3. **M1 EMA20 breakouts become unreliable** — price whipsaws around the EMA rather than trending cleanly through it

## When to consider a filter or pause

If price is approaching a key support level (like S1=$4,492.36) AND H1 RSI is < 20:

- **Wait for confirmation** — don't take the first M1 EMA20 breakout. Wait for a 2nd consecutive M1 close below EMA20 before entering SELL
- **Tighter SL** — use M1 5-bar swing instead of 15-bar (shorter lookback = less history from higher prices)
- **Or pause completely** — if price is within 5pts of S1, no entry until it either bounces off S1 or breaks clearly through

This is NOT a reason to add an RSI filter to V4.2 (we know that kills PnL). It's a **situational judgment call** the agent should make when analyzing the market before deploying.
